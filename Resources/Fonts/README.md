# Fonts

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono, by the [Braille Institute](https://www.brailleinstitute.org/freefont/). The MIT license in the repo root does not cover these files.

Copyright and license notice, as embedded in the font files:

> © 2020, 2024 Braille Institute of America, Inc., a 501(c)(3) Charitable Organization.
>
> Braille Institute of America, Inc. provides Atkinson Hyperlegible for use, without derivatives or alteration, to the public free of charge for all non-commercial and commercial work. No attribution required.

The files here are unmodified. `build.sh` copies them into `Spooterfy.app/Contents/Resources/Fonts`, and the app registers them at launch, so they don't need to be installed.
